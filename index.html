Sound familiar?

The contract's on your desk. And every call to a law firm starts the meter.

Big companies have a lawyer on the leadership team. It's called a General Counsel, and their job is to know the business well enough to see trouble coming.

Most growing businesses can't justify one full-time. So they go without, sign things they half understand, and call a lawyer once something has already gone wrong. By then it's expensive.

Acuity Green gives you that senior lawyer, for the time you actually need.

You sign knowing what you've agreed to.

Customer contracts, investor documents, supplier terms. Read by someone who knows what your business can live with.

You hear about the risk while it's cheap to fix.

We're there during the negotiation, the tender and the board paper, not after the dispute.

You get a straight answer you can act on.

Plain English and a clear recommendation. You won't get a 20-page memo you have to decode.

You stop carrying it alone.

Someone senior to sense-check the decision before you make it.
